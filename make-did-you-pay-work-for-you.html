Make Did You Pay? Work for You

Get the most from Did You Pay? with practical tips and recommended ways to use the app.

Tip 1

Location and Notification Settings

The recommended Location and Notification settings have been tested and are recommended for the most reliable experience, whether Did You Pay? is open, running in the background, or closed.

Using different settings may prevent reminders from being created when you visit a selected location or could result in reminders being missed.

From time to time, iOS may also display a message confirming that Did You Pay? has been using your location in the background. This is a normal Apple notification and simply confirms that the app is using the location permission you have granted.

To continue receiving reliable location-based reminders, select Always Allow. Choosing Only While Using may prevent reminders from being created when you visit a selected location while the app is running in the background or closed.

Tip 2

Why Set a Payment Location?

Did You Pay? already reminds you on a schedule until your reminder expires at midnight the day after your visit.

A Payment Location adds an extra reminder every time you arrive there while you have an active reminder.

For example, if you normally pay when you get home, set your home as your Payment Location. Whenever you arrive home with an active reminder, Did You Pay? waits 10 minutes, then sends you another reminder, regardless of the normal reminder schedule.

If you leave and return home again while the reminder is still active, you’ll receive another reminder 10 minutes after arriving. This helps ensure you’re reminded whenever you’re back somewhere you’re likely to make the payment.

Tip 3

App Status

The App Status card on the Home screen shows whether Did You Pay? is configured using the recommended settings from the Getting Started guide and these support pages.

When all the recommended settings are in place, the App Status is green and shows Ready.

If one or more settings need attention, the App Status is amber and shows Attention Needed, helping you identify what should be changed for the most reliable experience.

Tip 4

Responding to Notifications

Whenever you receive a Did You Pay? notification, you have three options:

  1. Tap and hold the notification to access the quick actions, then select Remind Me Again or I’ve Paid without opening the app.
  2. Tap the notification to open the app, then choose Remind Me Again or I’ve Paid.
  3. Do nothing. If you don’t respond, Did You Pay? will automatically schedule the next reminder until it expires or you mark it as paid.

Tip 5

Manual Reminders and Siri

You don’t have to visit a selected location to create a reminder.

If you need a reminder for any other payment, tap Manual Reminder on the Home screen or ask Siri to use Did You Pay?

Manual and Siri reminders work in exactly the same way as location-based reminders. You’ll continue to receive reminders until you mark the payment as complete or the reminder expires at midnight on the day after it was created.

Tip 6

Home Screen Widget

Did You Pay? includes a Home Screen widget that you can add to your iPhone. If you prefer, you can use the widget instead of the app icon.

The widget lets you see at a glance whether you have an active reminder, which location is currently selected, and the current App Status, without opening the app.

A Few Things to Know

Location Accuracy

Did You Pay? uses a radius around each selected location rather than a single point. Because location services rely on GPS and other signals, reminders may occasionally be triggered slightly before or after you arrive.

Privacy

Your selected locations, reminders and settings remain on your device. Did You Pay? does not upload or share your personal location history.

Battery Usage

Did You Pay? uses Apple’s built-in location services, which are designed to minimise battery usage while still providing reliable location-based reminders.
